💡 Tips for Learning from YouTube

1. Subscribe to channels: Hit the notification bell to stay updated on new educational content.

2. Create playlists: Organize videos by topic for structured learning.

3. Take notes: Pause videos frequently and write down key concepts.

4. Watch in series: Many channels have comprehensive playlists; watch them in order for better understanding.

5. Practice along: Follow coding tutorials and AI examples hands-on for better learning.

6. Watch at 1.25-1.5x speed: Save time while maintaining comprehension (or slow down if content is complex).

7. Combine resources: Watch different channels on the same topic to get multiple perspectives and explanations.
